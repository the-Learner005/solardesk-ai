
# SolarDesk AI

An AI-powered solar business management platform designed to streamline customer management, quotations, sales follow-ups, and business operations.

## Vision

SolarDesk AI aims to bring customer relationships, product management, quotation workflows, and AI-powered business intelligence into one centralized platform.

## Planned Modules

- Customer CRM
- Solar product and pricing management
- Quotation management and PDF generation
- Sales pipeline and follow-up management
- WhatsApp Business Platform integration
- AI-powered business assistant
- Sales analytics and reporting

## Technology Stack

- Frontend: React, TypeScript
- Backend: Node.js, NestJS
- Database: PostgreSQL
- ORM: Prisma
- Cache and Queues: Redis, BullMQ
- AI: LLM APIs, pgvector
- Deployment: Docker

## Architecture

The application will initially follow a modular monolith architecture.

PostgreSQL will serve as the primary source of truth.

## Project Status

Early development — project foundation and architecture setup.
